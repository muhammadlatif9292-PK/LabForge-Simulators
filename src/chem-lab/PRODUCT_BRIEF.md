## Users
  Primary: students
  Secondary: hobiest and teachers and chemists

## Problem
  Chemistry students often struggle to visualize the dynamic nature of chemical reactions beyond static equations. Traditional labs can be dangerous and time-consuming, while simple simulations often lack the mathematical rigor of real-time ODE-based concentration tracking.

## MVP Features (these 5 only)
  1. Mix chemicals, see real ODE simulation
  2. Live concentration chart
  3. Automatic safety warnings
  4. AI explanation of results
  5. Export JSON

## Non-Goals
  No user accounts, no mobile app, no 1000+ substances, no organics

## Success Metric
  A student can successfully perform a virtual titration and receive an AI-generated explanation that correctly identifies the equivalence point from the simulated data.
